OUR STORY

Grey Pitori is a South African lifestyle brand born in Pretoria, known by its people as Pitori.

Pitori is more than a city. It is home. It is legacy. It is where cultures meet, ideas evolve, and futures are built.

Grey is the colour between black and white. To us, it represents the intersection of people, cultures, perspectives, and ambition. In a city as diverse as Pitori, we believe greatness is found where differences meet.

Grey also symbolizes the journey between who you are and who you're becoming. It is the space where purpose is discovered, confidence is built, and identity is expressed.

Our visual identity is intentionally black and white. These colours represent two ends of a spectrum, while Grey represents the connection between them—the people, stories, and experiences that shape our community.

Grey Pitori creates clothing that reflects confidence, culture, and purpose. Every collection is designed to honour individuality while celebrating the spirit of Pitori.
